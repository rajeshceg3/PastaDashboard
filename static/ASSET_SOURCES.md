# Asset sources

The ten pasta SVG shape studies are original schematic illustrations made for this app, not photographs or exact culinary reference plates. Reuse follows this repository's license. No broken photo or its caption is presented as a loaded pasta photograph. Original data photo URLs are preserved for provenance, but not loaded by the catalog.

Map context: Natural Earth 1:110m country geometry, extracted from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson . Public-domain terms verified at https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Dataset information: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-admin-0-countries/ . Simplified educational map, not navigation. Catalog coordinates are inherited representative locations, not verified historical birthplaces.
